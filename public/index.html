"What OMORI mod should I play first?"


If you found this site, you probably asked this question! This site is designed to let you configure what you're looking for in an OMORI mod, and we'll show you which mods you'll like to check out first!


Scalar Preferences
📖 Story Focus Low High The focus the mod gives to its story.
🎮 Gameplay Focus Low High The focus the mod gives to its gameplay, mainly combat.
🎨 Artistic Focus Low High The focus the mod gives to its art and music.
🎭 Comedic Focus Low High The focus the mod gives to its humor and comedy.
💡 Vanilla Focus Low High The focus the mod gives to the original game; how similar to OMORI it is.
⌛ Playtime Low High The time is takes to complete the mod in full. These are rated relative to each other and the base game.

Restrictions
🌐 In Language? If the mod has a translation for the selected language. Note that some mods may have translations for only certain releases.




Recommended


This list is generated in ascending order from the following formula:
Variables: u = User Preferences, m = Mod Statistics, r = 100 * Every Failed Restriction
Match = 30 - Σ|m-u| - r
A higher number is a closer match.
